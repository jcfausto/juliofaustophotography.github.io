## In short

This website has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and sets no
cookies. Its fonts and images are served from the website itself. Personal data
is only processed to deliver the website, to answer your emails, and to fulfil
print orders, as described below.

## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P.
Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit a page, your
browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address,

participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's

## Email and print requests

If you write to me, for example through a "Request a print" link, I process the
data you send: your name, email address, the content of your message, and,
for print requests, the photograph and size you chose and your delivery
country. I use it to reply to you and, if you order a print, to prepare and
complete the order.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR for print requests and orders (steps
before and for the performance of a contract) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR for other
enquiries (my legitimate interest in answering messages).

My email is provided by Google (Gmail). Messages are therefore stored and
processed by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4,
Ireland, and may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which participates in
the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

I delete your messages when they are no longer needed. Records of completed
orders are kept for as long as tax and commercial law require (up to ten years,
§ 147 AO, § 257 HGB).

## Orders: production, delivery, and payment

If you order a print, I pass the delivery details needed to the print lab that
produces and ships the print, and I send you a payment request through a
payment service provider. Both process your data to fulfil the order, under
their own privacy policies. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

## YouTube videos

Some pages contain YouTube videos. They are not loaded until you press play.
Only then does your browser connect to YouTube (Google Ireland Limited), using
youtube-nocookie.com. YouTube can then process your IP address and other
data, and may store information on your device. By pressing play you consent
to this (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 25 (1) TDDDG); you can always watch the video
on YouTube instead. See Google's
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Links to other websites

This website links to other services, such as YouTube, Instagram, and
jcfausto.com. No data is sent to them unless you follow a link; from then on,
their privacy policies apply.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it
corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18),
to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to object to processing based
on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time, with
effect for the future. To exercise these rights, email me.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection
supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of
your residence or place of work.
